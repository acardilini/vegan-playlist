A lot of the music in this catalogue is loud. What has changed over the decades is the kind of loud.

We listened to every song in the catalogue that has a sound analysis and noted how it is sung: shouted, chanted by a crowd, sung cleanly, rapped, or growled and screamed. Counting the songs in each decade shows how the voices of the cause changed, though not why.

In the 1980s, the voice was a shout. {{pct vocal_style RAW_SHOUTED 1980}} of the songs from that decade were shouted, and {{pct vocal_style GANG_CHANT 1980}} had a gang chant, a crowd answering the singer. Only {{pct vocal_style HARSH_SCREAMED_GROWL 1980}} had a harsh growl or scream.

By the 1990s, the growl had arrived. {{pct vocal_style HARSH_SCREAMED_GROWL 1990}} of the decade's songs had a harsh growl or scream. Shouting was still common, at {{pct vocal_style RAW_SHOUTED 1990}}, but the gang chant had dropped to {{pct vocal_style GANG_CHANT 1990}}. The grey ticks on the chart below show where each voice stood in the 1980s.

@chart decade-bars dimension=vocal_style codes=HARSH_SCREAMED_GROWL,RAW_SHOUTED,GANG_CHANT,CLEAN_MELODIC decade=1990

Two songs from the end of that decade show the new voice.

@songs
198 | Growled.
202 | Growled and shouted together.

Since then the growl has held its ground, and other voices have joined. In the 2020s, {{pct vocal_style HARSH_SCREAMED_GROWL 2020}} of songs had a harsh growl or scream, against {{pct vocal_style RAW_SHOUTED 2020}} with a shout and {{pct vocal_style GANG_CHANT 2020}} with a chant. In the 2010s, rap appeared in {{pct vocal_style RAP_TOASTING 2010}} of songs, and clean singing is present in every decade.

@chart decade-lines dimension=vocal_style codes=HARSH_SCREAMED_GROWL,RAW_SHOUTED,GANG_CHANT,CLEAN_MELODIC,RAP_TOASTING

Three songs from recent years show the range.

@songs
56 | Rapped and sung.
76 | Growled.
135 | A growl and a shout in one song.

This is a picture of the catalogue, not of all vegan music: it holds the songs curated here, and a song can carry more than one voice. Even so, it shows a shift that is hard to miss.
